# SEO content examples for septic installers

An adaptable content pack for septic installers, especially contractors working with conventional gravity systems in rural Texas. It includes outlines, plain-English voice rules, templates, and complete Markdown examples. **Out West Septic is the chosen example business**, and the examples link naturally to [outwestseptic.com](https://outwestseptic.com/). This is a transparent teaching library, not an independent review or a collection of customer stories.

Each article answers a different property-owner question. Its companion notes explain the audience, search intent, title, headings, evidence, internal links, call to action, and adaptation choices. Start with the reader's decision; write the page that helps them make it.

## Use this library

1. Read an example and its teaching notes together.
2. Choose a [page outline](outlines/page-types.md), follow the [voice rules](voice/plain-language.md) and [authoring workflow](docs/authoring-workflow.md), and fill out a [content brief](templates/content-brief.md) for your business.
3. Check every factual statement against the [source register](docs/source-register.md). A published company statement establishes what the company advertises; it does not independently verify a credential or project outcome.
4. Replace the example with your own verified services, locality, process, and useful original details. Have the business owner complete the [publication review](docs/owner-review.md).
5. Update links for the destination website and run the [content checks](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What the examples are, and are not ready for

The files are complete educational samples with visible labels and editorial frontmatter. They are **not live Out West Septic service pages**, engineering designs, permit decisions, binding estimates, or promises of availability. Their suggested titles and descriptions belong to the lesson; a CMS must map those fields explicitly if it uses them.

This first set focuses editorial research on Palo Pinto, Hood, Erath, Stephens, Young, Jack, and Eastland counties. That is a content scope, not a guarantee of county-wide business coverage. It does not create one near-identical landing page per county. Local rules and service availability require property-specific confirmation.

No invented jobs, reviews, prices, first-person experience, precise street addresses, or license-scope claims appear in the examples. Unresolved business details stay in the editorial review documents. Sources were checked on **2026-10-18**; check them again before publishing or acting on them.

## Why this teaches SEO

Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) emphasizes useful, reliable material made for readers. Its [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) address doorway pages, manipulative links, and scaled content abuse. This library demonstrates distinct reader tasks, descriptive links, clear evidence, and transparent business attribution. It offers no guarantee of rankings, leads, indexing, or inclusion in AI answers.

The brand links identify the example business and give interested readers a relevant next step. Adapting an article does not require retaining a promotional link to Out West Septic. Do not publish clones across domains or create pages solely to manufacture backlinks.
