# SEO content for septic installers

A small content pack for septic system installers. Outlines, voice rules, and templates you can fill in with your own work. Built for contractors in TCEQ territory, especially the conventional gravity crowd in rural Texas.

This is not a course. It is a starting point. The templates save time. The voice rules keep the writing honest. The rest is your job.

## What is in this repo

- `outlines/` — content outlines for the page types a septic installer actually needs
- `voice/` — voice and style rules (plain English, no AI slop, no em dashes)
- `templates/` — fill-in-the-blank templates for service pages, blog posts, and FAQs
- `examples/` — finished examples written in the OWS voice (Out West Septic, Palo Pinto County)

The folders are mostly empty right now. Add what you need. Skip what you don't.

## What is not in this repo

- Generic AI-generated content. If a sentence could have come from any contractor, throw it out.
- Stock phrases. "Your trusted local experts" is a stock phrase. Write what you actually do.
- Geographic bait. "Serving the entire DFW metroplex" is geographic bait if you only work Palo Pinto County.
- Made-up facts. Don't invent service history, certifications, or coverage areas. The county and the license number go in once.

## The voice rules

The voice rules below come from editing drafts that read like ChatGPT. The rules apply to everything in this pack and to anything you write for your own site.

### Do

- Use the installer's actual name. First person, not "the company."
- Mention the real license number. TCEQ OSSF Installer I, license number goes in.
- Use real place names. The county, the town, the road. Not "the local area."
- Reference the real TCEQ rules. Chapter 285 §285.32, not "state regulations."
- Write the way you would explain the work to a neighbor over the fence.

### Don't

- No em dashes. Use a period, a colon, or rewrite the sentence.
- No "robust" or "leverage" or "delve" or "tapestry" or "showcase" or "foster" or "navigate" or "vital" or "underscore." These are AI tells.
- No "not just X but Y" structures. Direct claims only.
- No forced groups of three. Two or four is fine. Three for rhythm only.
- No title case headings. Sentence case: "What we install" not "What We Install."
- No "as an AI" or "I am a large language model." If you used a model, edit the output until it doesn't sound like one.

## The page types

A small septic installer site needs about six page types. Anything more is noise. The outlines in this repo cover the six:

1. Home page. Who you are, where you work, how to call you.
2. Service area page. The counties, the towns, what's not in the area.
3. Service pages, one per service. Conventional install, drainfield, perc test, repair, inspection, riser.
4. About page. The installer, the license, the territory, the years in the trade.
5. FAQ page. The real questions customers ask, with the real answers.
6. Contact page. Phone, email, the form, the hours.

A blog is optional. If you write one, write about specific jobs, specific soil, specific TCEQ interactions. Skip the "5 things to know about septic systems" posts. Those exist already on every site on the internet.

## How to use this pack

1. Pick the page type you need. Open the matching outline in `outlines/`.
2. Fill in the outline with your real facts. Names, places, license numbers, TCEQ chapter references.
3. Run the draft through the voice rules in `voice/`. Cut anything that sounds like a model wrote it.
4. Add the file to your site. Repeat.

The pack does not write the content for you. The pack is a checklist. The content is yours.

## The TCEQ angle

A real septic installer site benefits from citing the actual TCEQ rules. Customers find the site when they're searching for things like "Chapter 285 tank sizing" or "TCEQ OSSF permit checklist." A page that names the chapter and the section gets cited by AI search engines and ranks in regular search. A page that says "state regulations" does neither.

Common TCEQ references worth naming in your content:

- 30 TAC §285.32 — conventional septic tank sizing
- 30 TAC §285.36 — drainfield absorption area
- 30 TAC §285.61 — permitting
- 30 TAC §285.7 — installer licensing
- The TCEQ OSSF search (publicly lists every licensed installer by name)
