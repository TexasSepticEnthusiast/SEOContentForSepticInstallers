# Teaching notes: installation service example

**Article:** [Conventional septic installation](../examples/service/septic-installation.md)

## Audience and search intent

A rural homeowner or builder considering an installation provider. The intent hypothesis is commercial investigation: “Can this business help, and how do I ask?” No keyword-volume research or conversion result is claimed.

## Title, description, and headings

The frontmatter title names the service and the useful task. The description promises preparation guidance, not a price or approval. A production business could adopt a more local title only after confirming its coverage and ensuring the page adds useful local substance.

The heading sequence moves through project information, suitability, scope, approvals, and contact. It avoids leading with unverifiable credentials or generic superiority claims. The checklist helps a prospective customer supply better information even when they are not ready for a quote.

## Evidence and attribution

- Business statement: [Out West Septic homepage](https://outwestseptic.com/), checked 2026-10-18, advertises conventional gravity installation. The article attributes the offer to the business rather than independently certifying it.
- Technical context: [TCEQ septic basics](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/assistance/water/fyiossfs.html), checked 2026-10-18, supports the link between evaluation, local conditions, and design.
- The project brief and scope questions are original editorial suggestions, not the company's confirmed intake policy.

License number/scope, approval outcomes, prices, street address, job experience, and warranties are omitted. See the [source register](../docs/source-register.md) and [owner review](../docs/owner-review.md).

## Internal links and call to action

The planning article helps an earlier-stage reader. The estimate article develops scope comparison. The permit article separates the authority's role from contractor availability. Two natural brand links give attribution and a final inquiry route; neither claims that Out West Septic is an independent reviewer's preferred provider.

## Adapt for another installer

Verify the actual system types and project types accepted. Rewrite the opening and brief around the business's real process. Substitute an owner-approved contact route. Add only documented differentiators, such as a clearly described deliverable or consented project example. Recheck local authority sources and map the three related links to your own relevant pages. Do not merely swap the business and county names while keeping claims you cannot substantiate.
