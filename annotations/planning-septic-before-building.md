# Teaching notes: planning septic before a rural land purchase

**Article:** [Planning septic before buying rural land](../examples/guides/planning-septic-before-building.md). These notes are separate from the sample article.

- **Audience:** A prospective rural Texas land buyer or owner arranging a new home, before the site layout is committed.
- **Search intent:** Learn what to check before buying or preparing land that may need septic. The article helps the reader organize a decision rather than choose a system from a search result.
- **Suggested title:** Planning septic before you buy rural Texas land
- **Suggested meta description:** Plan septic before buying rural Texas land: organize parcel records, use soil maps wisely, and coordinate the house, driveway, and evaluation.
- **Distinct purpose:** A property file, two-layout planning exercise, and written decision brief. Detailed permit applications, installer comparisons, and estimate line items belong in companion examples.

## Why the outline works

The headings follow the decisions in order: assemble information, interpret map limitations, preserve layout options, obtain the appropriate evaluation, and record the next commitments. The table gives the reader something to take into a real conversation. The fictional layout comparison is labeled as a planning exercise; it is not represented as a customer project.
