# Teaching notes: permit questions example

**Article:** [Septic permit questions](../examples/guides/septic-permit-questions.md)

## Audience and search intent

A property owner who needs to identify the right office and understand what to ask before scheduling work. The editorial intent hypothesis is procedural information, not a request for engineering advice or an instant approval decision.

## Title, description, and heading logic

The title promises questions, which the article can deliver. The description accurately limits local examples to Palo Pinto and Hood. The headings move from parcel identification to official entry points, responsibilities, scheduling assumptions, and the contractor discussion. No fee or turnaround figure is inserted to make the page look comprehensive.

The two-county table teaches how local evidence can change a page. It is a compact resource inside one guide, not a reason to generate seven similar county landing pages.

## Sources and claim boundaries

Checked 2026-10-18:

- [TCEQ permit guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfpermits.html): local authority and potentially stricter local programs. Exemptions are not simplified into an acreage rule.
- [Palo Pinto County Public Works](https://www.co.palo-pinto.tx.us/page/PublicWorksDepartment): published application/planning/evaluation/fee components and preconstruction authorization.
- [Hood County OSSF page](https://hoodcounty.texas.gov/departments/environmental_health/onsite_sewage_facilities_%28ossf%29.php): stated permit categories and application resources.

The questions and recordkeeping suggestions are original editorial organization. They are not a claim that every county requires each listed item. The article does not establish jurisdiction for any individual parcel. See the [source register](../docs/source-register.md).

## Links and CTA

The planning and estimate links answer adjacent decisions without duplicating their checklists. The final brand link gives the chosen example business a relevant inquiry route while keeping authority responsibilities explicit.

## Adapt for another installer

Research the actual local authority from primary sources. Verify the packet and date, then summarize only the details needed for the reader's next step. Remove this table if it is irrelevant to your audience. Do not retain county names to attract unrelated traffic. Confirm the provider's real role in submissions and inspections before describing it as company policy; keep unverified fees and schedules outside public copy.
