# Service-page worksheet

Editorial template only. The prompts below must be resolved before writing customer-facing copy; they are not statements about Out West Septic or any other business.

## Before drafting

Record the business's verified service, accepted project types, relevant qualifications, area-confirmation process, and contact route. Attach a source or owner confirmation for each. Keep unresolved items in the [content brief](content-brief.md).

## Draft sequence

1. **Title and opening:** Name the service and explain the reader's immediate next step. Avoid unverified superlatives, availability, or price claims.
2. **Project fit:** Explain the verified scope and any confirmed exclusions that affect the inquiry.
3. **Information to gather:** Supply a short checklist relevant to this service, with missing information clearly distinguished from required professional work.
4. **Responsibilities:** Explain only confirmed company steps. Present unconfirmed scope questions as questions, not included services.
5. **Related guidance:** Link to a distinct planning or comparison resource where it helps the reader.
6. **Call to action:** Use the verified contact route and say what information makes the next conversation useful.

## Companion annotation

Explain the audience, search-intent hypothesis, metadata choices, heading order, claim sources, link purposes, and adaptation requirements. Study the [installation example](../examples/service/septic-installation.md) and its notes before creating another one.
