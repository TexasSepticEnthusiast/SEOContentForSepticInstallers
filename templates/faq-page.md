# FAQ worksheet

Editorial template only. These prompts help build answers; they are not claims about the example business.

## Choose questions with a reason

List the actual question, its source, and the decision behind it. A customer question may inform the topic without publishing the customer's identity or words. When choosing a question without customer evidence, mark it as an editorial hypothesis.

## Write each answer

Give a direct answer in plain language. Add the minimum context needed to prevent a misleading impression, then explain who can resolve any property-specific unknown. Link to the relevant service page or official source instead of repeating an entire guide.

For “Do you work in my county?”, confirm actual coverage and explain how to check a property. For “How much will it cost?”, use only owner-approved pricing or explain what information a scoped estimate needs. For “Can I use a conventional system?”, explain the role of the appropriate evaluation and authority rather than diagnosing the parcel.

## Check before publication

- Is this the business's actual answer, or does it need owner approval?
- Does any technical or regulatory statement have a current primary source?
- Could a reader mistake a conditional answer for a guarantee?
- Does the related link resolve a useful next question?
- Does another page already answer this better?

Add a short annotation on question selection, search intent, source limits, and links. Do not promise FAQ rich results or AI citations from the formatting.
