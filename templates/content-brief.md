# Content brief template

Editorial worksheet only. Complete this outside the article body; unresolved fields must not become public facts.

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Reader and decision | Who needs help, and what should they be able to do after reading? |
| Search intent hypothesis | Service inquiry, preparation, comparison, or local procedure; identify any actual evidence |
| Distinct contribution | What does this page help with that existing pages do not? |
| Page title and H1 | Accurate, human-readable description of that task |
| Suggested description | Plain summary, without price or result promises |
| Heading sequence | The order of decisions, rather than a list of keywords |
| Business facts | Public source plus owner verification required |
| Official facts | Direct authority URL, date checked, supported claim, limits |
| Practical output | Checklist, comparison worksheet, or preparation steps |
| Internal links | Destination page, anchor text, and why it helps here |
| Call to action | Appropriate next step; verified destination and availability limits |
| Unresolved details | Facts that must remain outside article copy until verified |
| Original material | Approved observations, photos, or examples and their permissions |
| Publication review | Reviewer, date, link mapping, source recheck, and checks completed |

Use [the workflow](../docs/authoring-workflow.md) and [owner review](../docs/owner-review.md) before transferring a draft to a website.
