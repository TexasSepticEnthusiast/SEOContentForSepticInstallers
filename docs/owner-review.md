# Business-owner review before website publication

The library is suitable for reviewing as educational examples. Publishing any sample as the business's own service copy requires the following checks. Open items are editorial tasks, not assertions inside the articles.

- [ ] Confirm the business name and the current public inquiry route.
- [ ] Confirm which installation projects the business accepts, who performs evaluations/design work, and which services it refers elsewhere. An installer credential is not evidence of every related qualification.
- [ ] Confirm property-level service availability. The seven-county editorial focus does not establish coverage of every address.
- [ ] Reconcile public website differences about the base location and service-area extent before inserting an address, radius, or complete county list into copy.
- [ ] If adding a credential, check the regulator's current record, exact class, holder, status, and authorized scope. This library does not independently validate a license number or legal scope.
- [ ] Verify any added phone number, hours, physical address, price, financing offer, schedule, warranty, or contract term directly with the business.
- [ ] Review the sample inquiry checklist and workflow as suggested customer preparation. Do not imply the business has adopted an unconfirmed contractual process.
- [ ] Ask the applicable permitting authority to resolve property-specific requirements. Check official sources again; do not rely on a neighboring county's rules.
- [ ] If adding real project photos or narratives, obtain permission and document the actual work. Remove identifying homeowner/property details unless approved for publication.
- [ ] Replace repository-relative links with the intended live URLs. Keep useful source links and date the review.
- [ ] Map title/description fields deliberately in the CMS. Keep the visible educational label when reproducing the sample as a lesson; replace it with accurately attributed company copy only after owner review.
- [ ] Retain the MIT notice when redistributing substantial portions of this library. Check permissions separately for third-party text, photographs, and business marks.

## Deliberately absent from the public examples

No prices, fee schedules, guaranteed approval timelines, exact base address, testimonials, job counts, performance statistics, credential number, or first-person work history have been supplied. If useful later, collect evidence and add only the specific fact the evidence supports.

Do not fill these gaps with plausible values. A good page can explain the next decision without them.
